    How you play

    Hold Erase with one thumb and rub a wall with your other finger. Release to draw again. On PC, right-drag to erase. Hold WASD to aim Stevie’s paper throws; release to return to auto-aim. Erasing costs no ink and recovers a portion of the paid ink in healthy walls.
