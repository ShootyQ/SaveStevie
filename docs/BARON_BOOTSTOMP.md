# Bootstomp — wave 10 boss

Status: active wave-10 encounter in Alpha 0.1.111. Wobblechomp is parked: his implementation, artwork and regression checks remain intact.

Artwork: `assets/art/concepts/baron-bootstomp.png`. Wide, horizontally oval blue crayon monster, enormous yellow boots, no crown or royal headwear; the encounter uses whole-character drawn animation frames.

Animation catalogue: `assets/art/bootstomp/animations.json`. Open `docs/bootstomp-preview.html` through the project server to inspect connected sequences, individual actions, and erasing references. Full production notes are in `docs/BOOTSTOMP_ANIMATIONS.md`.

## Fight direction

- Use the existing eraser-held paper dent under his telegraphed landing foot. At least 80% preparation makes him stumble and fall, without tearing through the sheet.
- A successful stomp sends a fast paper ripple toward Stevie. The first player wall halves its damage once; further walls cannot reduce it again.
- On a fall, expose five drawing patches. Each takes three deliberate erasing passes, with persistent progress. Target one completed patch per fall, with time toward another for quick players.
- Start with twice King Doodle-Doom’s current add spawn count. Do not buff Doodle-Doom as part of this change.
- Shoe scrape removes sections of cover along a telegraphed strip.
- Tantrum shuffle moves the boss and shoves enemies between stomp attempts.
- Bootlace flick launches a nearby monster. Draw across the stretched lace to snag it before launch; otherwise block the passenger in flight. Do not scrape away a prepared footprint during the same committed stomp.

## First playable tuning

Ordinary gameplay erasing requires six pixels of brush travel before applying an erasing segment. Slow pointer samples accumulate from an anchor. Clicking or tapping without travel does not count as rubbing. The boss additionally requires 18 pixels of real travel per patch swipe, a reversal or fresh stroke before another swipe, and 0.6 seconds between hits.


The stomp charge lasts 3.25 seconds; keep holding through landing. Falling exposes
patches for 3.2 seconds. All fifteen swipes must be completed across openings.
Paper balls, wall abilities and external crowd control cannot skip this counter.
A missed stomp deals 14 base damage, or 7 after crossing its first wall, before
Stevie’s armor. The helper interval is half Doodle-Doom’s current interval, with
at most fourteen owned helpers alive; spawning pauses during intro and openings.

A real defeat still unlocks Circle Studies and preserves the existing saved
wave-10 victory flag. The dev encounter shortcut does not award that progression.
Sound cues currently reuse existing effects pending dedicated recordings.

Validation: `node tests/validate.cjs` includes the boss regression checks;
`node tests/browser-bootstomp-fight.cjs` exercises real mouse and two-finger touch
input, persistent erased artwork, and a wall-softened ripple. No desktop installer
is generated by these checks. Balance remains for player playtesting.
