# Continuous paper wear and openings

The page uses a sparse 8px wear field rather than spawning spaced hole images. A moving eraser wears its swept capsule. One second of local rubbing opens cells; short pointer-event gaps retain the previous 0.1s grace, and holding a motionless brush cannot wear indefinitely. Repeated samples share one frame budget. Very broad gestures distribute time over their spatial extent, so one long sweep does not immediately open the whole page.

Open cells form connected regions. Growth is allowed next to existing holes; rubbing paper bridges joins regions. Only exposed cell edges contribute to the outline. Smoothed deterministic contours, a subtle white fibrous lip, and a quiet shadow replace thick brown borders. The same continuous honey-colored desk and aligned grain are drawn beneath every opening. White thinning, fibres and small cracks show progress without percentage labels. No spontaneous growth occurs.

The authoritative wear field, connected-component IDs and hit tests are shared by visuals and monsters. A connected opening cannot supply both ends of a shortcut. Entry checks use actual open cells with a small body tolerance, not a hole-center circle; exits need a safe open point closer to Stevie. Large openings search for a roomy exit near Stevie. Moving bumps naturally emerge if their route crosses another opening; active bump rubbing retains its two-second forced-emergence counter. Boss/specialist exclusions, the 30% ordinary entry chance, warnings, surface immunity and paper-ball interaction remain.

Rip-Raff and forced emergence open the same wear field, so their openings can join player tears. New isolated hardhat holes retain the prior 24-region guard; player wear has a finite 65,536-cell budget instead of spacing dead zones. The fort, page boundaries and existing wax safeguards remain protected. New waves/runs reset the field; resize translates its origin and encounter geometry together. Paper state is run-local; permanent save formats do not change.

Wear/connected-region/contour work happens on mutation. A cached canvas, capped at 2048 pixels on its longest axis, is rebuilt only when visible wear or geometry changes and then drawn as a single layer. Connected outlines are not recomputed every render. Exit-point searches use a bounded 64-entry cache, invalidated by new openings or resize, so a large tear is not rescanned for each entering monster. Drawing consumes no combat RNG or simulation state. The cell budget bounds storage and geometry work.
