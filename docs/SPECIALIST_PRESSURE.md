# Specialist pressure — Alpha 0.1.70

From wave 13, regular spawning uses 48% of the original spawn rate rather than the previous 80%: another 40% reduction. The two timed chapter groups shrink to Chonks/Pew Pew and Yank/Gribble. Splitter families remain parked. Rip-Raff rejoins the roster at wave 14 with its existing one-living-worker cap.

Catapults set up along the playable outer edge, using top/bottom positions on portrait phones and left/right positions on wide landscape screens. The erasable machine stays visible. Successful setup calls two off-page Gribble volunteers from wave 13; an empty queue can call another pair. Each builder recruits at most six, and all specialists share ten recruits per wave. Volunteers head to the catapult, wait, and remain vulnerable to normal walls/effects and paper balls. Removing the machine releases waiting recruits to normal movement. No new recruits arrive after the timed wave or during boss entrances/fights.

When Rip-Raff's bomb completes an exit at least 45 pixels closer to Stevie than its entrance, it calls up to five off-page Gribbles. They approach the entrance faster, then take the completed shortcut without the ordinary 30% roll. Ordinary monsters retain their 30% chance, and bosses never enter holes. The existing underground damage, erasing, emergence warning and recovery rules remain.

Yank targets finished walls while the player draws elsewhere; active unfinished strokes are excluded. Hook reach increases from 90 to 110 pixels. A full pull moves a wall up to 108 pixels instead of 72, bounded to the page. Circle metadata moves with the wall. Erasing the rope and existing damage/freeze/stun counters still apply.

Hijacked Sneaky followers pause confused for two seconds at their route's endpoint. Their short stun survives retirement of the old route, giving the player a real enclosure opportunity. Specialist-directed recruits are excluded from Sneaky rallies while on their job.

Turrets are available during the timed portion of boss waves and while clearing ordinary survivors. The boss warning/entrance immediately clears cannons and pending offers; direct boss spawning clears them too. No turrets can be constructed during the entrance or fight. Enclose and its existing boss immunities remain.

This is a first balance pass for player feedback. Required validation, focused reinforcement/route/turret checks, desktop/phone catapult erasing and circle-tree browser checks, and the web build passed.
