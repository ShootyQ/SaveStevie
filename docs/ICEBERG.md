# Legendary Frost: Iceberg

Taking Legendary Frost Ink grants four normal Frost ranks plus Iceberg for that run. Ordinary ranks never unlock it. Existing and future walls have 4× maximum HP; current HP also multiplies, preserving damage percentage. Repeat legendary picks add Frost ranks without multiplying durability again. Removing Frost removes Iceberg and divides surviving walls' HP back down, preserving damage percentage. New runs clear the unlock; new waves retain it.

Ice shelves form over 0.7 seconds, with pale layered ice, irregular crystals and damage cracks below 75% and 40% HP. Facets are sampled by length and capped at 80 per wall. Rendering does not use combat RNG or mutate simulation. Reduced motion shows fully formed shelves immediately.

Wall lifetime, collision thickness, ink cost, eraser cuts/refunds, and boss wall-clearing remain unchanged. No extra freeze or boss stun bypass. Turret walls receive durability but retain their cannon appearance; Wobble ricochet bumpers retain their appearance. Existing circles and line-extension HP use the same multiplier; HP is not re-multiplied every frame.
