# Legendary Electric: Tesla

Legendary Electric grants four ordinary Electric levels plus a run-only Tesla unlock. Ordinary Electric ranks never unlock it. Repeated legendary picks add ranks without duplicating the network. Replacing Electric, a new run, wave cleanup and boss entrances clear its charge/bolts; the unlock persists between waves.

Eligible living wall strokes host one coil per 100px, minimum one and maximum three. At most 24 coils render/target across the network. Turret walls and Wobble ricochet bumpers are excluded. All coils share a two-second recharge. A fully charged network holds charge until an eligible target appears. A volley chooses at most eight distinct closest enemies within 160px of a coil (plus enemy radius), hitting each once for 4× the current normal Electric source hit damage. At Electric level 4 this is 31.2 damage each. Overlap/erased fragments cannot multiply hits on one target. No catch-up volleys after a long frame.

Normal contact chain lightning remains unchanged. Tesla does not add chain hits, stuns or shock resets. Electric immunity, shield protection, boss ability immunity, underground safety and airborne/held enemies are respected. Damage uses normal combat handling and kill rewards. Existing boss immunities stay meaningful.

Copper coils, charging halos and orbiting sparks show readiness. Up to eight brief layered zigzag bolts and endpoint rings show a volley. Rendering is pure and deterministic, does not consume gameplay RNG, and respects reduced motion. Pause/menus/hidden pages stop charge and effect aging. Resize translates transient bolts; coils follow their actual walls.
