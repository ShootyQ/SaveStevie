## Art direction carried forward

- Use only monsters actually in the game, referenced against their existing artwork.
- Keep Stevie recognizable and preserve the rough pencil, crayon and school-notebook identity.
- Favor specific, uneven handmade details over polished matching icons, uniform decorative distress or overly neat lettering.
- The previously generated cover is a starting concept, not a complete set of Steam-ready exports.
- No image generation or screenshot capture is requested in this documentation step.
