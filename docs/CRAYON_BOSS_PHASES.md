# Count Crayon — wave 15

Phase one is implemented. Phases two and three below are design proposals.

## Phase one: erase and return

Count Crayon keeps moving while cycling through three warned abilities:

- Scribble Snakes: two coloured wax trails grow toward Stevie. Erase across a trail to stop its tip and leave a coloured stub. A real player-drawn pencil line connecting the surviving wax to the boss sends a melted-wax packet back along the drawing. Freehand ink works too. Each trail can return only once.
- Wax Splats: three marked blobs arrive after a one-second flight and hatch at four seconds into small wax doodles. Erase the landing blobs before hatching; surviving helpers behave as ordinary melee enemies.
- Fresh Colour: a 1.2-second warning precedes redrawing the six shield sections. Erasing a section opens it. Wax packets must reach an erased section, giving 14% maximum-health damage. Shield openings remain until he redraws them. Paper balls and direct elemental hits help while exposed; closed shielding reduces ordinary damage to 8%.

Limits: four trails, three unhatched blobs, six helpers and four returning packets. Trails last fourteen seconds; cut stubs remain for up to nine seconds. Erasing wax gives no ink refund and briefly protects the erased wax area from paper rubbing. Ordinary bare-paper rubbing still works.

Movement runs during attacks. The body silently erases walls along its swept path without wall explosions, heals or refunds. Control immunity prevents freezing, stunning, slowing, launching or sticky-note interruptions. Portrait/desktop use a perimeter route; landscape pages bridge above the fort where there is room, with a fluid flank route on very short screens. Damage comes from warned snakes and hatchlings; the boss does not deal body-contact damage.

Until later phases are built, defeating this phase completes wave 15 normally.

## Later proposal: phase two — more lines

- Forked Tongue: branching snakes, with a shared erasable junction.
- Crayon Train: wax creatures ride tracks and skid off when track sections are erased.
- Patch Patrol: a doodle assistant repairs interrupted tracks, including a route the player has redirected toward the boss.

Keep bounded active tracks; add decisions about their connections rather than filling the screen.

## Later proposal: phase three — the masterpiece

- Living Portrait: a large boss doodle whose mouth, hands and feet each enable an attack; erasing a section removes that ability.
- Colour Rush: visible colour packets feed the portrait; interrupt or reroute those packets toward the boss.
- Emergency Touch-Up: warned ghost outlines reveal which erased section he will restore next.

The player chooses what to erase for safety and what to preserve for retaliation.
