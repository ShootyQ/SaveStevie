Opening the animation sheets…

Left/right means the side you see on screen. Foot-switching and clipped-hand cells are excluded from playback. Erase clips show one independently damaged part at a time. They are art references; persistent combined damage belongs in the future boss renderer.

The paper dent, travelling crease and scuffs are separate environmental studies. Characters use whole drawn frames—no separately animated mouths, eyes or limbs. Reduced-motion preferences pause playback; use the scrubber to inspect poses.
