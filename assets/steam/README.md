All exports are opaque RGB PNGs at the exact upload dimensions. Each composition was generated separately; the small capsule limits the cast to Stevie and the red grunt for readability. Header, Main and Vertical also feature Basil, King Doodle, Zoom Nugget and the armored tank. Page Background is deliberately dark and quiet through the center. Shortcut Icon uses a close-up of Stevie and his pencil; Steam accepts the PNG and generates its ICO. Final export sizing makes only the tiny aspect-ratio adjustment needed to fit the supplied dimensions.

Generated with OpenAI image generation, using existing game art as references. Rough handmade styling describes the appearance, not the production method.
